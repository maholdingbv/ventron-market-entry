What we are building toward

Three development principles. One goal: optimum performance in practice.

VENTRON is focusing its development on three areas that determine how a suction excavator performs in the real world.

01

Performance

The pickup speed of a turbine excavator. The pulling power of deep vacuum.

A concept that combines high airflow with deep vacuum—developed for fast material pickup and the reserve to keep pulling as resistance increases.

02

Operator Experience

Controls and interactions developed around how operators perceive, decide and work, informed by current insights from the market.

03

Engineered for a longer life

Engineering and material choices focused on limiting wear, corrosion and product degradation—for limited maintenance and repair costs and a long economic life.
